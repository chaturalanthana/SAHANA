# UAT script

Status: prepared 31 October 2026. Stakeholder execution is not recorded.

Use a seeded non-production society. Repeat the path in English, Sinhala, and Tamil on the web shell at 320px and at desktop width, and on the mobile shell for sign-in and emergency.

1. Sign in as the society administrator. Confirm the society name and that another society's records are absent.
2. Change the contact phone with a reason. Confirm the previous value remains in history.
3. Record a member as `applied`, move the member to `active`, and add a family profile. Confirm the family profile has no login.
4. Import two rows that share a member number. Confirm nothing new is saved and the rejection is reported.
5. Publish a welfare rule, submit a claim, and confirm the stored eligibility does not change after a later rule version.
6. Post a balanced receipt, reverse it, and confirm the original journal is still posted.
7. Accept a suggestion and confirm the original text is unchanged.
8. Submit an emergency with type and phone only, then close it. Confirm the screen says SAHANA does not replace emergency services.
9. Verify one ESG source, leave another unverified, and confirm only the verified source is in the total.

Do not treat a green automated test run as this script having been signed.
